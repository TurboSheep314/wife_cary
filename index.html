Mother's Day Wife Carry

Folks, it's happening. The Cold Spring Park Wife Carry for Mother's Day. Tremendous event. Maybe the greatest soccer team event of all time -- people are saying that. Strong husbands, incredible wives, unbelievable athleticism. Nobody carries wives like this team, nobody.

We're talking obstacles, speed, balance, determination. Total chaos in the best possible way. Some people train for marathons -- our guys train to carry their wives through mud and grass while trying not to collapse. Very high-level competition. Very serious.

Come out to Cold Spring Park and watch the moms get the appreciation they deserve -- and watch the dads completely gas themselves after 40 yards. It's going to be fun, loud, competitive, and frankly, beautiful.

Happy Mother's Day to all the amazing moms. We love the moms. The best people. See you at the race.

How it works
